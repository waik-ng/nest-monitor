Nest Monitor

A personal, single-user tool that monitors one Google Nest thermostat for likely HVAC faults — including short cycling — and sends a Gmail alert when one is found.

What it does

Nest Monitor runs on a Raspberry Pi 4 on a cron schedule (every 2 minutes). On each poll it reads the thermostat's current status through Google's official Smart Device Management (SDM) API and logs it locally:

  • Room temperature and heating / cooling setpoint
  • Thermostat mode (Heat, Cool, Heat/Cool, Off) and HVAC status (Heating, Cooling, Off)
  • Humidity and connectivity status

From those readings it reconstructs heating and cooling cycles and looks for patterns that suggest a problem:

  • Short cycling — a completed heat or cool run of 10 minutes or less
  • Rapid cycling — 4 or more cycles started in one hour
  • Long run with no progress — running for 90 minutes or more without moving meaningfully toward the setpoint
  • Thermostat unreachable — repeated failed API polls, or the thermostat reporting itself offline

When a fault is found, it sends an email alert via Gmail to the owner. It only reads thermostat status — it never changes the thermostat's settings.

Who uses it

This app is for personal use only, by its owner, on their own Nest thermostat and their own Raspberry Pi. It is not offered to the public, has no other users, collects no data from anyone else, and shows no advertising.

Data

Thermostat readings are stored locally on the owner's Raspberry Pi in a SQLite database, and alert emails are sent to the owner's own Gmail address. No data is sold or shared with any third party except Google, whose SDM and Gmail APIs are required for the app to function. See the Privacy Policy for details.
