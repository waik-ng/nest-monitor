Privacy Policy — Nest Monitor

Effective date: November 8, 2026
App: Nest Monitor — a personal, single-user tool for monitoring one Google Nest thermostat.

What data is collected

Nest Monitor accesses the following data about the owner's own Nest thermostat, through Google's Smart Device Management (SDM) API, only after the owner grants access with their own Google account:

  • Room (ambient) temperature and heating / cooling setpoints
  • Thermostat mode and HVAC status (Heating, Cooling, or Off)
  • Humidity and connectivity status

It also uses the owner's Gmail address to send alert emails to the owner, via Gmail SMTP.

No other personal data is collected. The app has no other users, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

How data is used

The thermostat data is used only to log the thermostat's status, detect likely HVAC faults (such as short cycling, rapid cycling, or a long run with no progress toward the setpoint), and email an alert to the owner when a fault is detected. The app only reads thermostat status; it never changes the thermostat's settings.

Where data is stored

Readings are stored locally on the owner's Raspberry Pi in a SQLite database (nest_data.db). They are not uploaded to any server operated by the app, and they are not shared with any third party except Google, whose SDM and Gmail APIs are required for the app to function.

Data sharing

Data is not sold, rented, or shared with any third party for any purpose. The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data retention and deletion

Readings are kept locally on the owner's Raspberry Pi until the owner deletes them. The owner can delete all stored readings at any time by deleting the nest_data.db file, and can revoke the app's access to their Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops all future data collection.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the app owner at the support email listed on the app's Google Cloud OAuth consent screen.
